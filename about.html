Our story

From a rented hall to a home for 2,400 dancers

Pulse Studio was founded in 2019 by Priya Nair and Karthik Raman with three weekly classes and eleven members. Word spread the way good music does, and by 2022 we had outgrown the hall.

Today we run 48 classes a week across Zumba, aerobics, step and low-impact formats, all designed around one promise: you will leave happier than you arrived.
